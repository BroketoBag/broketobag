Canada Child Benefit (CCB)

Up to $7,787/year per child under 6. Tax-free. Apply through CRA My Account.

CRA.ca →
🎓

RESP + CESG Grant

Government adds 20% on first $2,500/year per child — $500 free annually. Lifetime max $7,200.

📊

TFSA (2025: $7,000/year)

Tax-free growth. Withdrawals don't affect benefits. Unused room carries forward.

CRA Guide →

First Home Savings Account (FHSA)

$8,000/year tax-deductible, $40,000 lifetime. Tax-free for first home.
